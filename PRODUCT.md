# Product

## Register

product

## Users

InternKim serves employees and administrators operating an internal AI workspace. Its shared company page is an external storytelling surface for intended recipients who know its password.

## Product Purpose

InternKim brings company operations, collaboration, attendance, and AI-assisted work into one dependable system. The shared company page turns administrator-approved company data into a safe, current-feeling view without exposing internal records.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and human. The interface should communicate confidence through clarity while making the organization feel active and inhabited.

## Anti-references

Avoid ornamental activity, generic dashboard theater, surveillance-like employee monitoring, and external pages that reveal full identities, emails, exact attendance times, unpublished tasks, or other internal operational detail.

## Design Principles

- Show real signals instead of decorative claims.
- Preserve privacy through aggregation and deliberate publication.
- Turn existing company records into verifiable evidence without exposing their private operational context.
- Keep internal workflows familiar and efficient.
- Let the shared company page tell a concise, human story.
- Make freshness and publication timing visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard access. Do not depend on color alone for data meaning. Respect reduced-motion preferences and ensure activity visualizations remain understandable without animation.
