internkim is an AI coworker a company runs on its own computer. It reads the
company's messenger, does what people ask of it **under their own identity**,
and keeps every file, task and memory on hardware the company owns.

## Three sentences

A company keeps one computer on. The hardware does not matter.

People reach their messenger and their files from any network through the web
app. No connection is ever made into that computer.

Nothing the agent does is anonymous. It runs as the Linux user of whoever
asked, and the records it leaves carry that person's name.
