  <h2 className="internkim-overview__section-title">Why it exists</h2>

  <div className="internkim-overview__prose">

A small company that wants AI at work hits the same walls. Chat AIs know nothing about the company, so every request starts from scratch, and once you have the answer a person still does the work. The company's work is scattered across an attendance system, a calendar, a messenger, a drive and spreadsheets, so an AI cannot carry a job from one to the next. And keeping AI costs down means choosing, wiring up and running models, which takes a developer.

</div>

  <div className="internkim-overview__reasons">
    <div className="internkim-overview__reason">
      <span className="internkim-overview__path-title">No starting from scratch</span>
      <span className="internkim-overview__path-description">
        With the work and its files in one place, the AI knows the whole company's
        context. Whoever asks starts from what the company has already built up.
      </span>
    </div>
    <div className="internkim-overview__reason">
      <span className="internkim-overview__path-title">No seat for every employee</span>
      <span className="internkim-overview__path-description">
        A pricey AI subscription per employee is simple, but you pay for the people
        who barely use it. InternKim picks the right model for each job under one
        company key, and you pay for what is used.
      </span>
    </div>
    <div className="internkim-overview__reason">
      <span className="internkim-overview__path-title">A data room that fills as you work</span>
      <span className="internkim-overview__path-description">
        The contracts, quotes and reports made along the way are filed in the data
        room. When an investor asks for documents, you send one link.
      </span>
    </div>
  </div>
