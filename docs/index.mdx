  <h2 className="internkim-overview__section-title">Why it exists</h2>

  <div className="internkim-overview__prose">

AI chat tools are clever but know nothing about your company. Who is off today, which contract is open, what was promised last week: you paste it in every time, and once you have the answer, a person still does the work.

A company's work is scattered across an attendance system, a calendar, a messenger, a drive and spreadsheets, so an AI cannot carry a job from one to the next. The AI each tool adds knows only what happens inside that tool.

InternKim builds the workspace and the AI together. With the work in one place, the AI knows the company's context and does the work itself, with the permissions of whoever asked. The company keeps the permissions and the data.

</div>
