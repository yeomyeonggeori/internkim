A role contains `readableCategories`, an array of codes. `F` includes all
finance children, including ones added later. `FS` grants financial
statements alone. Multiple active grants combine; there are no deny overrides.
Reader roles grant reading. Company administration and document editing are
separate permissions. Filing eligibility does not limit permission scopes:
both parent and intermediate codes can appear in a role.

| readableCategories | Access |
|---|---|
| `["F"]` | Finance and every current or future child |
| `["FS"]` | Financial statements only |
| `["FS", "FP"]` | Financial statements and payroll |

A category without children can hold documents. A category with children
groups their documents and remains a valid permission scope.

Company administrators assign roles to members, internal circles, external
emails or explicitly public audiences. A verified email recipient must accept
the invitation. They gain no company membership, circle or host filesystem
identity. An expired or revoked grant stops new reads. Original file downloads
require `canDownload`; text previews follow read access. A downloaded copy
cannot be recalled, and issued file links expire within ten minutes.

Publication requires an explicit public grant. It never publishes `X`.
Editing a role immediately changes every active recipient's scope. Adding a
child immediately extends existing parent grants. Review those recipients
before changing either. Category codes and ancestry are stable.

New members receive the member role; administrators created with a company
also receive leadership. Later changes to company administrator status do not
assign or revoke a reader role. Manage these grants separately.

## Code-protected links

An active member creates a link by choosing a reader role within their own
grants. Every role is eligible, including leadership;
`X` follows the same role and creator permissions. Its default lifetime is
three days; the available lifetimes are defined by
[`dataRoomLinkLifetimeHours`](https://github.com/yeomyeonggeori/internkim/blob/main/web/src/lib/data-room/links.ts).
The database enforces a seven-day ceiling.

The app generates a six digit code, displays it once and stores its salted
password hash. The recipient enters the code and acknowledges the versioned
confidentiality notice. This creates a guest session for up to one hour,
bounded by the link's expiration, without creating a company member. Ten code
attempts per network address in fifteen minutes temporarily lock further
attempts. A link also has a shared ceiling of one hundred attempts per window.

Every database read checks the link, current reader role and creator's current
permissions. Role expansion cannot exceed the creator's access. The creator
or a company administrator can revoke a link. An open page loses further
access and clears its displayed documents on its next session check.
Original download URLs expire within one minute. Already downloaded files
cannot be recalled.

Settings → Company replaces a member's reader roles atomically. The roles a
member holds are also their circles on the company host. The confidentiality acknowledgment records
its notice version and time; any separate confidentiality agreement remains
the company's responsibility.

Moving a document into another category changes its readers.
