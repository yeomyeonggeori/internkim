---
title: Handing InternKim work
description: Where and how to ask, what it can take on, and when it checks with you first.
icon: MessageCircle
---

Ask InternKim the way you would ask a colleague. There are no commands and no
forms: say what you want, and attach a file if it helps.

## Where to ask [#where]

- **The web app's messenger.** Talk to InternKim one to one, or call it into a team channel.
- **The company messenger app.** The same conversation continues in the messenger app on your phone or desktop.
- **In a channel.** Where several people talk, mention InternKim and it answers that message.

Every one of these is the same conversation. Ask on the web and read the answer on your phone.

## What it can take on [#what]

| Area | Ask it like this |
|---|---|
| Attendance and leave | “Clock me in”, “Take next Friday afternoon off”, “How much leave do I have left?” |
| Calendar | “Find 30 minutes with Park Example on Thursday afternoon”, “What's on tomorrow?” |
| Work | “Ask Choi Sample to review the contract”, “Summarise my work this week” |
| Documents | “Make a deck from this”, “Make a quote PDF”, “Summarise this spreadsheet” |
| Mail | “Which of today's emails need a reply?”, “Draft a polite no to this one” |
| Company files | “Find last year's investment agreement”, “Make a link with the documents for the investor” |
| Customers | “Add this business card as a contact”, “Show the customers we met last month” |
| On a schedule | “Every Monday at 9, send me last week's summary”, “Remind me of the meeting at 3” |
| Looking things up | “What's the exchange rate this week?”, “Will it rain in Seoul tomorrow?” |

Send a picture or a file along and it works from what is in it. Mail needs your
mail account connected once in settings.

## When it checks with you first [#asks-first]

Before anything hard to undo, it asks the person who asked. You get a
notification and approve or decline in the conversation. When it is unsure
what you mean, it asks rather than guesses.

## Whose permissions it uses [#whose-permissions]

It works with the permissions of whoever asked. What that person cannot see, it
cannot see either, and what it leaves behind carries their name. Things only an
admin may do have to be asked by an admin.

## Memory [#memory]

Tell it something like “I prefer meetings in the morning” and it keeps that in
mind for later requests. A person's memory is used only for that person, and a
team circle's memory only for that circle's members.
