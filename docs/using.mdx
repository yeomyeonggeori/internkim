Ask InternKim the way you would ask a colleague. There are no commands and no
forms: say what you want, and attach a file if it helps.

## Where to ask [#where]

- **The web app's messenger.** Talk to InternKim one to one, or call it into a team channel.
- **The internkim app.** On a Mac, an iPhone or an Android phone, the app opens the same messenger and notifies you of replies.
- **The company messenger app.** The same conversation continues in the messenger app on your phone or desktop.
- **In a channel.** Where several people talk, mention InternKim and it answers that message.

Every one of these is the same conversation. Ask on the web and read the answer on your phone.

## What it can take on [#what]

| Area | Ask it like this |
|---|---|
| Attendance and leave | “Clock me in”, “Take next Friday afternoon off”, “How much leave do I have left?” |
| Calendar | “Find 30 minutes with Park Example on Thursday afternoon”, “What's on tomorrow?” |
| Work | “Ask Choi Sample to review the contract”, “Summarise my work this week” |
| Documents | “Make a deck from this”, “Make a quote PDF”, “Summarise this spreadsheet” |
| Mail | “Which of today's emails need a reply?”, “Draft a polite no to this one” |
| Company files | “Find last year's investment agreement”, “Make a link with the documents for the investor” |
| Customers | “Add this business card as a contact”, “Show the customers we met last month” |
| On a schedule | “Every Monday at 9, send me last week's summary”, “Remind me of the meeting at 3” |
| Looking things up | “What's the exchange rate this week?”, “Will it rain in Seoul tomorrow?” |

Send a picture or a file along and it works from what is in it. Mail needs your
mail account connected once, under Mail settings on the Mail page.

## While it works [#while-it-works]

A bubble beside InternKim's picture lists the steps it is taking, and the
answer takes its place. You can keep writing meanwhile. Each message you send
during a task is weighed once against it: a question about progress is
answered, a correction changes the work under way, and "stop" or a new
instruction in its place ends it. A separate request waits and is answered as
its own once the task ends.

`/stop` stops the latest task in the conversation at once, and `/stop-all`
stops every task you have running. Scheduled runs stay scheduled. A task that
could not finish what you asked ends as failed, and the reply says what was
left undone.

## When it checks with you first [#asks-first]

Before anything hard to undo, it asks the person who asked. You get a
notification, and the question appears in the conversation as a card whose
options only you can press. You can also answer in your own words. Scheduled
work is asked about once, when you schedule it, and its runs then go ahead
without asking again. When it is unsure what you mean, it asks rather than
guesses. [The tools page](/tools#approval) says which calls ask.

## On your own computer [#your-computer]

With the internkim desktop app open, you can ask for something done in an app
on your own computer. The app asks once, after you first sign in, whether
InternKim may use the computer, and the switch under This computer in
Settings → Notifications changes the answer later. On a Mac the app also needs
the Accessibility permission. Scheduled runs never use your computer.
[How it works](/tools#desktop).

## Briefings [#briefings]

Every morning at 8:00 InternKim sends you a direct message with today's
schedule, the work under way and what is planned. Every Friday at 12:00 it sends
a short report of your week: what you finished, what is in progress and what
comes next. A week with no work sends no report. Both follow the company's
time zone, and Settings → My InternKim changes their times, the report's day,
or turns either off.

## Whose permissions it uses [#whose-permissions]

It works with the permissions of whoever asked. What that person cannot see, it
cannot see either, and what it leaves behind carries their name. Things only an
admin may do have to be asked by an admin.

## Memory [#memory]

Tell it something like “I prefer meetings in the morning” and it keeps that in
mind for later requests. A person's memory is used only for that person, and a
team circle's memory only for that circle's members.
