---
title: Sharing tasks
description: Letting someone outside the company read the tasks of chosen businesses, by email.
icon: Share2
---

An administrator invites an email address to read the tasks of one or more
businesses: the labels the company files its work under, kept on the task
page's Definitions tab. The invited person signs in with that address and sees
those businesses' tasks, read-only and unmasked. An invitation names at least
one business, and nothing else is shared by it.

## What the guest sees [#shown]

| Shown | Never shown |
|---|---|
| title, status, dates, type, size | notes and who asked for the task |
| the names of the people taking part | anyone's email |
| a parent task in an invited business | a task linked to a CRM deal, or a calendar event |
| how many tasks each status holds | anything from a business that was not invited |

The answer is assembled in the database from the invited businesses alone.
Another business's task, its name, its count and the people working on it are
never sent, masked or otherwise. The board is live: a task added to an invited
business appears at the guest's next read, and a task moved to another
business leaves it. Each status shows its hundred most recently changed tasks
and says how many more it holds.

## Inviting [#inviting]

On the task page, **Invite outside** opens the invitation dialog. Enter the
address, choose the businesses, and send. The agent does the same with
`task_invite`, after approval.

The email is the guest's sign-in. Following its link signs them in and opens
the shared tasks; the page then offers to register a passkey, so a later visit
needs no email at all. Without one, the guest enters the address again and
receives a code. Only an invited address can request a code: the guest page
never creates an account.

Inviting an address that already holds an invitation replaces its businesses
and sends the email again. **Send again** in the list does that with the same
businesses. A business an open invitation names cannot be removed from the
task vocabulary until the invitation is revoked.

## Revoking [#revoking]

**Revoke** in the list, or `task_invitation_delete`, ends the invitation. The
guest sees nothing from their next read. Accepting creates no company member,
circle or host account, so there is nothing else to remove.
